Special scheduling rules

Choose subjects that should be prioritised early in the day and subjects that must be taught in two consecutive lessons. Double lessons can start at any valid consecutive pair from Lesson 1 through the final lesson pair of the day; they are balanced across the week rather than restricted to morning periods. Subjects with related concepts or skills must not follow one another in consecutive periods across any grade. This includes language-to-language lessons, related sciences and Mathematics with Physics. Creative Arts receives a strong preference for the period immediately before a break or lunch, without making otherwise valid timetables impossible. The solver also balances each grade’s morning and afternoon lesson load across the week.
